Advertising: One Community contains NO advertising of any kind. It shows no
ads, banners, sponsored or promoted content, and no promotions for other apps
or products. It includes no advertising SDK or ad network and does not use the
advertising identifier (IDFA), SKAdNetwork or AdServices. The Age Rating
answer "Advertising: No" is therefore correct. Every screen shows only the
resident's own society information (visitors, bills, notices, events, menus).

Tracking: One Community does not track users. It has no third-party analytics
SDKs and does not share any data with data brokers or for advertising. Data is
used only to provide the app's features to the resident's own society, so the
app does not use App Tracking Transparency. In App Privacy, no data type is
marked as used for tracking or for advertising.

One Community is used by residents of a registered residential society.
Sign-in is by mobile number and one-time password (OTP), so a self-service
account cannot be created. We have set up a demo society for review:

1. On the first screen, choose "Marzi Demo Residency (App Review)".
2. Enter the mobile number 9999999001 and tap "Send code".
3. Enter the code 0000. (This demo number never receives an SMS; the code
   is fixed.)

After sign-in you will see a visitor waiting at the gate with Approve /
Reject buttons. This is the app's core feature: security logs a guest at
the gate and the resident decides from their phone.

Maintenance bills are shown for information only. Residents pay at their
society office, and the app has no payment feature. Canteen pre-orders are
paid at the society canteen; the app takes no payment.

Location is requested only to attach the resident's position to an
Emergency SOS; it is never used in the background. The demo society has no
security staff, so pressing SOS alerts no one.

Account deletion: Profile > Settings > Delete account.

Devices: this is an iPhone app (portrait only). On iPad it runs in iPhone
compatibility mode.
