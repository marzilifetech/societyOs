## RESIDENT APP — `com.marzi.resident`

**App name** (≤30 chars)

```
Marzi: Society & Community
```

**Short description** (≤80 chars)

```
Society app for residents: visitor entry, maintenance dues, complaints & notices
```

**Full description** (≤4000 chars) — rewritten to clearly state purpose + features

```
Marzi is a community management app for residents of gated societies, apartment
complexes and housing communities. It connects you with your society's security
gate, management office and neighbours, so day-to-day community living is
handled from one place instead of phone calls, registers and WhatsApp groups.

After your society adds you as a resident, you log in with your phone number and
get access to the features below.

VISITOR & GATE MANAGEMENT
- Pre-invite guests and share a digital gate pass with a QR code.
- Approve or reject visitors in real time when they arrive at the gate.
- Get instant alerts for deliveries, cabs and service staff, and approve them
  before they reach your door.
- View a log of who has visited your home.

MAINTENANCE & PAYMENTS
- See your monthly maintenance dues and outstanding balance.
- Pay dues securely online and download receipts.
- Track your full payment history in one place.

COMPLAINTS & SERVICE REQUESTS
- Raise a complaint or maintenance request (plumbing, electrical, housekeeping
  and more) and add photos.
- Track each request from "raised" to "resolved" with status updates.

NOTICES, POLLS & EVENTS
- Read official notices and announcements from your society management.
- Vote in community polls on society decisions.
- See upcoming society events and RSVP.

EMERGENCY SOS
- Send an emergency alert to your society's security and help desk with one tap,
  along with your location, so help can reach you quickly.

MORE EVERYDAY SERVICES
- Order from the society canteen and view the menu.
- Raise concierge and help-desk requests.
- Manage your household profile, family members and property details.
- Book community services and amenities offered by your society.

BUILT FOR EVERYONE
- Clean, simple design with large, readable text.
- Optional Senior Mode with bigger text and higher contrast.
- Real-time push notifications for approvals, payments and announcements.

Marzi is intended for residents whose society or apartment community uses the
Marzi platform. You will need to be registered by your society to use the app.

Questions or help: support is available through the app and at societyos.app.
```

## STAFF APP — `com.marzi.staff`

**App name** (≤30 chars)

```
Marzi Staff: Society Duty
```

**Short description** (≤80 chars)

```
Duty app for society staff: attendance, tasks, visitor entry & gate management
```

**Full description** (≤4000 chars) — rewritten to clearly state purpose + features

```
Marzi Staff is a workforce app for the on-duty staff of gated societies and
apartment complexes — security guards, gate supervisors, housekeeping,
maintenance and facility teams. It replaces paper registers and phone calls with
one app to manage attendance, gate entries, assigned tasks and emergencies.

Your society registers you as a staff member. You log in with your phone number
and see only the features relevant to your role.

ATTENDANCE & SHIFTS
- Check in and out at the gate. Attendance is verified using your location
  (geofence) so it is recorded only when you are on the society premises.
- View your assigned shift timings and hours worked.

VISITOR & GATE MANAGEMENT
- Log walk-in visitors and record their details at the gate.
- Scan a resident's QR gate pass to verify an expected guest instantly.
- Send entry requests to residents and record their approval or rejection.
- Keep a clear record of everyone entering and leaving.

TASKS & WORK ORDERS
- See tasks assigned to you (housekeeping, maintenance, patrols and more).
- Update task status as you start and finish work.
- Capture photo proof of completed work, stamped with time and location.

EMERGENCY SOS
- Raise and receive emergency alerts so the whole on-duty team can respond
  together, quickly.

LEAVE & TEAM
- Check your leave balance and apply for leave from your phone.
- Read notices and updates from society management.

BUILT FOR THE FIELD
- Simple, fast interface designed for use on the move.
- Real-time push notifications for new tasks, gate requests and alerts.
- Works for a range of staff roles across the society.

Marzi Staff is intended only for staff members of societies that use the Marzi
platform. You must be registered by your society to use the app.

Questions or help: support is available through the app and at societyos.app.
```
